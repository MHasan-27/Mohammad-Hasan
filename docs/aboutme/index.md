## Defending an Engineering Decision

To defend an engineering decision means grounding every design choice in empirical evidence, clear trade-offs, and physical constraints rather than personal preference. It requires proving *why* one solution was selected over another based on data, governing physics models, cost, and manufacturability. 

In my work, I approach this using a structured problem-solving framework:
1. **Define** the core technical problem.
2. **Measure** baseline performance and system metrics.
3. **Analyze** root causes and mechanical behavior.
4. **Improve** the design through iterative prototyping and testing.
5. **Control** the process with clear documentation and standards to ensure repeatable results.
